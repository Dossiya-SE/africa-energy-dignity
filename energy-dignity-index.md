# Energy Dignity Index

## Status

Concept-development stage. No composite index is approved yet.

## Required design sequence

1. Define each dimension.
2. Select indicators.
3. Define units and directionality.
4. Establish minimum-service thresholds.
5. Normalize indicators.
6. evaluate weighting alternatives.
7. Test compensability.
8. Validate against real service outcomes.
9. Conduct equity and sensitivity analysis.
10. Publish limitations.

## Critical warning

A high aggregate score must not be allowed to hide failure in a life-critical dimension. Minimum thresholds should be considered for healthcare, water, safety and basic household service.
