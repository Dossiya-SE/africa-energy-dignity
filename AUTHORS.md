## Contributor recognition

Africa Energy Dignity preserves contributor attribution through Git history, pull requests, issue records, release metadata and CRediT-compatible contribution roles.

Contributors are credited for the work they actually perform. Founder attribution does not replace or remove legitimate collaborator credit.

## AI-assisted development

AI-assisted systems may support drafting, coding, testing and research organization under human direction.

AI systems are not listed as authors or accountable contributors. Accepted work remains subject to human review and repository validation.
