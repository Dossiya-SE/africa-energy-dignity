# Country Case Methodology

## Selection criteria

- Severity and character of energy-access constraints
- Availability and quality of data
- Productive-use opportunity
- Institutional readiness
- Climate and infrastructure-resilience relevance
- Replicability
- Local partnership potential
- Feasibility within the demonstration period

## Case-study stages

1. Country baseline
2. Priority problem definition
3. Stakeholder and system mapping
4. Demand and capability assessment
5. Technology-option screening
6. Scenario modeling
7. Optimization and tradeoff analysis
8. Deployment design
9. Verification and validation
10. Reproducibility package
