# Optimization Model

## Initial conceptual score

`Score = costs + delays + environmental impacts + dependency + implementation risks − reliability − productive benefits − sovereignty − resilience − social benefits`

This expression is conceptual. It must not be implemented until variables, units, normalization rules, weights, constraints and validation criteria are approved.

## Core constraints

- Energy balance
- Generation-capacity limits
- Network-flow limits
- Storage state-of-charge dynamics
- Reliability thresholds
- Critical-service continuity
- Affordability limits
- Capital availability
- Construction capacity
- Workforce availability
- Supply-chain constraints
- Land and water safeguards
- Safety standards
- Local-content requirements
- Deployment deadlines
- Minimum data quality

## Required model outputs

- Preferred alternatives
- Pareto-efficient alternatives
- Constraint violations
- Sensitivity results
- Uncertainty ranges
- Binding constraints
- Tradeoff explanations
- Reproduction metadata
