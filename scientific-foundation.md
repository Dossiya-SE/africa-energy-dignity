# Scientific Foundation

## Central research question

How can Sub-Saharan African countries rapidly achieve universal, reliable and productive energy access while strengthening affordability, resilience, local capability, environmental sustainability and long-term energy sovereignty?

## Energy-dignity construct

`Energy Dignity = f(AC, RS, AF, QS, PU, EQ, LC, RE, SO)`

| Code | Dimension |
|---|---|
| AC | Physical availability and access |
| RS | Reliability and continuity |
| AF | Affordability |
| QS | Quality and adequacy |
| PU | Productive-use capability |
| EQ | Equity and inclusion |
| LC | Local capability and maintainability |
| RE | Infrastructure resilience |
| SO | Energy sovereignty |

## Scientific rule

The construct is provisional until each dimension has:

- A precise operational definition
- One or more measurable indicators
- A data source
- A unit or scale
- A direction of improvement
- A treatment of uncertainty
- A documented aggregation rule
- A validation strategy

## Evidence classes

- Observed
- Published
- Derived
- Assumed
- Scenario
- Expert judgment
- Unverified

No class may be silently converted into another.
