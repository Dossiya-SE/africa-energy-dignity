# Literature Review Protocol

## Purpose

Build the scientific evidence base for energy dignity, sovereignty, rapid energy deployment, productive use, reliability, resilience and energy-system planning in Sub-Saharan Africa.

## Protocol stages

1. Define review questions.
2. Register databases and search strings.
3. Define inclusion and exclusion criteria.
4. Deduplicate records.
5. Screen titles and abstracts.
6. Retrieve full texts.
7. Extract evidence using a fixed schema.
8. Verify page-level evidence anchors.
9. Appraise methodological quality.
10. Synthesize findings, contradictions and gaps.
11. Record uncertainty and unavailable evidence.
12. Publish a reproducible review log.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Do not invent articles, evidence, values, columns or rows.
- Keep extraction separate from interpretation.
- Anchor factual extraction to the source.
- Mark unavailable information explicitly.
- Preserve the original study context and units.
- Record model assumptions and limitations.
- Require independent review for high-impact claims.
