# Project Charter

## Project

Africa Energy Dignity — Open Energy Sovereignty Modeling and Rapid Deployment Engineering for Sub-Saharan Africa.

## Problem statement

A grid connection alone does not guarantee reliable service, affordable use, productive capability, critical-service continuity, local maintainability or strategic control. Existing planning approaches can also separate scientific modeling from procurement, construction, commissioning and measurable human outcomes.

Africa Energy Dignity addresses this gap through one reproducible system connecting:

`Evidence → Decision → Engineering → Deployment → Verification → Productive Impact`

## Mission

Build an open scientific and engineering platform that enables rapid, reliable, affordable, productive, resilient and locally governable energy-system planning and deployment.

## Primary users

- National and subnational public institutions
- Energy-system planners
- Engineers and utilities
- Researchers and universities
- Communities and productive users
- Development-finance and investment institutions
- Local manufacturers and technical enterprises

## In scope

- Access, adequacy, reliability and affordability
- Grid, mini-grid and stand-alone pathways
- Productive-use and critical-service demand
- Generation, network, storage and demand options
- Geospatial prioritization
- Lifecycle cost and environmental performance
- Reliability and climate resilience
- Local capability and technology transfer
- Energy and data sovereignty
- Deployment time, readiness and implementation risk
- Monitoring, verification and continuous improvement

## Initially out of scope

- Real-time control of operating national power systems
- Financial advice or investment guarantees
- Replacement of statutory engineering approval
- Unsupported prediction presented as measured evidence
- Country recommendations without country-specific validation

## First 90-day outcome

A functioning, documented foundation that can run a verified baseline model and display the first country-level scenario prototype.

## Success criteria

- Reproducible data lineage
- Explicit assumptions
- Verified equations and code
- Measurable rapidity indicators
- Documented uncertainty
- Validated dashboard outputs
- Accountable work ownership
- Selected demonstration case
