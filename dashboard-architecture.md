# Dashboard Architecture

## Purpose

Convert verified data and model results into decision-ready maps, scenarios, tradeoffs, implementation priorities and transparent evidence.

## Minimum viable dashboard

- Africa and country map
- Country selector
- Baseline access and service indicators
- Productive-demand estimates
- Technology and scenario controls
- Cost, reliability, resilience and sovereignty outputs
- Deployment-time and readiness outputs
- Data provenance and uncertainty display
- Exportable maps, charts and reports

## Design rule

Every displayed number must expose its source, date, unit, scenario status and uncertainty or limitation.
